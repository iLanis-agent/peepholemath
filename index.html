Peephole math

A peephole drilled at the wrong height serves nobody, and one drilled without measuring the door serves a draft. Peephole math turns the household's heights into eye levels and a verdict on a second lower viewer, the door's thickness into the right barrel length, and the hall's geometry into the span the viewer actually takes in. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Height plan - adult and child heights become labeled eye levels, and the plan says when a second lower viewer earns its drill.
  • Barrel plan - door thickness becomes a standard, long or extra-long barrel on the labeled range.
  • View plan - distance from the door and a labeled coverage ratio become the span the viewer takes in.

Labeled norms

Labeled norms: eye level at 93% of height, a 130 cm single-viewer band, barrels at 35-55, 55-75 and 75-100 mm, and a 0.4 coverage ratio for a wide-angle viewer - all illustrative, since posture, door construction and viewer models move the numbers. Drilling a door is permanent; measure the door, check the manufacturer's range, and rent-friendly doors deserve a landlord's yes first.
